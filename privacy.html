Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026

QuickBooks Bookkeeping Helper is a private internal tool used by the business owner to review and diagnose bookkeeping data from QuickBooks Online.

Information accessed

When authorized by the business owner, the tool may access QuickBooks Online accounting information such as company information, chart of accounts, reports, transactions, balances, customers, vendors, and related bookkeeping records.

How information is used

Information is used only to help the authorized business owner review bookkeeping, identify accounting inconsistencies, prepare diagnostic summaries, and monitor books for unusual or incorrect activity.

Storage

OAuth tokens and retrieved accounting data are stored locally on the business owner's computer or private infrastructure. The tool is not a public SaaS product and does not sell, rent, or share accounting data.

Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties except as directed by the authorized business owner or as required by law.

Security

The tool is intended for private use. Access should be limited to trusted devices and accounts controlled by the business owner. Tokens can be revoked at any time through Intuit account/app settings.

Data deletion and disconnection

The business owner may disconnect the application through Intuit app settings and may delete locally stored tokens or exported data at any time.

Contact

For questions about this private internal application, contact the business owner/operator.
